Verdict / stop condition

The finite host fixture supports preservation of the tested order/multiplicity, weak-list pruning, returned ownership and normal-path drop behavior. This is not permission to integrate: actual owning-crate guard/cfg typechecking remains unresolved. Five fewer physical lines alone would not justify this. The useful seam is that the same retain decision both keeps a weak entry and transfers its ONE successful upgraded Arc into the result. That coupling is easy to accidentally split into a liveness probe and a second upgrade; a named helper keeps the operation together without hiding which domain lock is owned. The interface is smaller than either loop and exposes exactly the mutable collection and owned result. It does add one navigation step and a generic symbol for only two callers; keeping the loops is defensible if maintainers prefer local explicit code. No universal adoption, maintenance savings or performance gain is established.
