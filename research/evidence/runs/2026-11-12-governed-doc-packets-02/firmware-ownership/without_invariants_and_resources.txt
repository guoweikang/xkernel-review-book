## purpose
Provide the device-tree backend's structural and property interpretation capabilities.

## owned_capabilities
Own structural access, property decoding, node status and device-tree address translation.

## non_goals
Do not own kernel registration, IRQ mapping, MMIO mapping or DMA allocation; controller-specific register roles and interrupt encodings belong to drivers.

## dependency_direction
Keep OS resource acquisition in existing provider contracts rather than importing those responsibilities into the device-tree library.

## delegation_interfaces
The historical design names FdtNode interpretation methods and the backend-neutral FwNodeOps contract; do not infer a CPU-specific interface from later changes.

## configuration
The design describes optional alloc for address collection and an optional fwnode backend feature; the complete build feature closure has not been validated here.

## exceptions
OF/IRQ/PCI and boot helpers outside the scoped migration remain tracked debt; their existence is not blanket approval for new interpretation duplication.
